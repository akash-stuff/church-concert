How to reserve your seats

Four short steps. Your confirmation and seat number are sent to your email and to WhatsApp, so either will get you through the door.

  1. Register

    Name, contact details and date of birth. Attendees must be 18 or older.

  2. Verify your details

    We send one code to your email and your WhatsApp. Enter it, or reply to the message.

  3. Choose your seat

    Pick one seat from the plan. It is held for you the moment you confirm.

  4. Bring your reference

    Your reference arrives by email and WhatsApp. Show either one at the door.

Good to know

There is no charge, ever

This is a free church event. Nobody will ask you for card details on this site, and there is no payment step. If a page ever asks you to pay, it is not us.

Family and friends

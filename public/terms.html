Terms and conditions

These terms cover reserving a seat for a Night of Worship concert at Grace Community Church. Admission is free — no payment is ever collected, on this site or at the door.

1. Who may register

You must be 18 years of age or older to hold an account. Children and young people under 18 are welcome at the concert, but their seats must be reserved by an adult who adds them as guests on the booking and who accompanies them on the night.

2. Your account

Give us details that are accurate and current, including an email address and a WhatsApp number you can read today — both are used to send your verification code and your ticket. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what is done through your account. Tell us straight away if you think somebody else has it.

3. Reserving seats

  • Seats are free, limited, and allocated on a first-come basis.
  • A reservation is confirmed only once your WhatsApp number has been verified and you have received a booking reference.
  • There is a limit on how many seats one account may hold for a concert. It is shown on the seat plan when you book.
  • Reservations are for the named guests on the booking. They may not be sold, advertised or transferred for payment of any kind.

4. Cancellations and no-shows

If you can no longer come, please cancel from your dashboard so the seat can go to somebody else. We may release seats that are unclaimed 15 minutes after the published start time, and we may cancel a booking that appears to be duplicated, automated or made in somebody else's name.

5. On the night

Bring your booking reference or the QR code from your ticket — on your phone is fine. A steward checks you in at the door and issues a wristband. Please arrive in good time; latecomers are seated at a break, where there is one. Doors, running time and any changes to the programme are at the church's discretion.

6. Messages we send you

By registering you agree to receive the messages needed to run your booking: your verification code, your ticket and confirmation, changes to the concert, and a reminder before the evening. These go by email and WhatsApp. We do not sell your details, and we do not send marketing from third parties.

7. Your data

We collect your name, date of birth, gender, address, email address, mobile and WhatsApp numbers, an emergency contact, and the details of any guests you add. We use them to manage seating, to reach you about the concert, and to look after people on the night. Records are kept while your account is open and for a reasonable period afterwards for our own accounting and safeguarding. To see, correct or delete what we hold, write to the church office.

8. Photography and recording

The concert may be photographed, recorded or streamed for church use. If you would rather not appear, tell a steward on the night and we will seat you away from the cameras.

9. Conduct

The church is a shared space. We may refuse admission to, or ask to leave, anyone behaving in a way that puts others at risk or disrupts the evening. Please follow the directions of stewards, particularly regarding exits and accessible seating.

10. Liability

Admission is free and offered in good faith. We are not liable for personal belongings, for travel costs, or for a concert being postponed or cancelled for reasons outside our control. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms — for example if the booking rules change. The version on this page is the one that applies. If a change is significant we will say so in the email that goes out before the concert.

12. Contact

Questions about a booking, your data, or these terms: contact the Grace Community Church office, or reply to any of the emails we have sent you.
